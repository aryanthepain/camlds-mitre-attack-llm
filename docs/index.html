Simple 3-Step Process

How the AI Solves the Problem

Instead of expecting human analysts to memorize thousands of hacker techniques, the system automates triage through three seamless steps.

1

Network Alarm Rings

The firewall or intrusion sensor spots suspicious traffic and generates a raw log message containing signatures, ports, and timestamps.

Input: Raw Suricata IDS Log
2

AI Searches 626 Tactics

The system converts the alarm into dense mathematical vectors and searches the official MITRE ATT&CK encyclopedia to identify similar attack patterns.

Engine: Vector Search & Local LLM
3

Instant Actionable Report

The security analyst receives a plain-English explanation, the exact attacker technique ID, severity rating, and recommended defensive next steps.

Output: Verified Threat Report

Real-World ROI

Why Companies Need This

Automating first-line alert triage solves the cybersecurity industry's biggest bottleneck: the severe shortage of specialized human analysts.

⏱ 90% Faster Response

Reduces incident triage time from 20+ minutes of manual log searching down to under 1 second of automated AI classification.

🛡 Eliminates Alert Fatigue

Filters out background network noise and elevates only critical threats that map to confirmed adversary attack stages.

💸 Zero Cloud Cost

Runs completely locally using open-source sentence embeddings and local models (Ollama), keeping private company logs 100% secure.
